JunYeong Park

I'm a Ph.D. student in the Department of AI Convergence at Dankook University in Korea, advised by Prof. Sang-Il Choi in the MLPA Lab. I'm currently a visiting researcher in the School of Computer Science at the University of Birmingham, hosted by Prof. Hyung Jin Chang (Vision Group).

I work on computer vision and multimodal learning. My research covers gaze following, which infers what a person is attending to, and medical image analysis, with a particular interest in building datasets that make these problems measurable. Earlier, I worked on audio-language models built on large language models.
